Last Updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2077 Bot (“Bot”, “2077”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use or interact with the Bot on Discord.

By using 2077 Bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Depending on the features you use, 2077 Bot may process the following types of information:

When necessary for the operation of the Bot, we may process:

- Discord User ID.
- Discord Server ID.
- Discord Channel ID.
- Discord Role ID.
- Discord username or display name.
- Discord avatar information.
- Information related to commands used with the Bot.

We generally use Discord IDs as technical identifiers rather than relying on usernames, since usernames and display names can change.

1.2 Messages and Command Data

Depending on the enabled features, the Bot may process:

- Commands sent to the Bot.
- Arguments or parameters provided with commands.
- Messages required for moderation or other configured features.
- Information necessary to execute a requested command.

We do not intend to permanently store the contents of ordinary messages unless a specific Bot feature requires such storage.

1.3 Server Configuration

If a server administrator configures 2077 Bot, we may store configuration information such as:

- Server ID.
- Channel IDs.
- Role IDs.
- Enabled or disabled features.
- Moderation settings.
- Custom prefixes or command settings.
- Other configuration values required by the Bot.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by 2077 Bot may be used to:

- Provide and operate Bot features.
- Execute commands requested by users.
- Store server configurations.
- Provide moderation and server-management features.
- Maintain user-related statistics or progression systems.
- Prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized use.
- Diagnose technical problems and errors.
- Improve the reliability and functionality of the Bot.
- Maintain the security of our systems.

We do not use information for purposes unrelated to operating, maintaining, securing, or improving 2077 Bot unless otherwise stated.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases, configuration files, logs, or other storage systems used to operate 2077 Bot.

The exact storage period depends on the type of information and the purpose for which it is required.

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide requested functionality.
- Maintain server configurations.
- Prevent abuse.
- Resolve technical issues.
- Meet legitimate operational or legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the feature and technical requirements.
